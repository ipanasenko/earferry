# EarFerry Sample Crossings

Ten original spoken-word videos for the public EarFerry demonstration feed.

Each MP4 has a plain black 1280×720 video track and AAC narration. The corresponding
source narration is in `scripts/`, while `manifest.json` contains upload metadata.

The clips are deliberately understandable without visuals. Upload them to an
EarFerry-owned YouTube playlist and let the daily sample-feed job choose one at
random, excluding the video selected on the previous day.

## Branded series

The upload-ready series uses the `*-branded.mp4` files and metadata in
`branded-manifest.json`. Each episode runs for roughly two to three minutes and
uses neural narration, the complete EarFerry lockup, Inter typography, a gently
rocking ear, drifting waves, and a narration-reactive waveform.

The earlier plain-black videos remain alongside them for comparison only.
